Lebanon

Lebanon is a country in the levant region of the middle east. It has one of the richest histories in the world, and origin to some of the most important figures in history; mostly due to it being the middle of the old world. It is also home to some of the most iconic symbols prevelent today.

Things to know when planning a trip to Lebanon

  1. Lebanon's first language is Arabic, however French is widely spoken too, and English a little less.


  2. It is widely known that Lebanon is one of the few countries where you can ski and swim within the same day.


  3. Lebanon has some of the best natural wonders, ranging from having no desert unlike the surrounding countries; as well as having underground marvels such as the many grottos.


  4. Lebanon's cuisine is unmatched in terms of variety, flavor, and especially love. Read more abt Lebanese cuisine on the Cuisine page.


  5. Lebanon has history that goes back over 4000 years and most sights here have their own historical importance.
