Lebanon's Wonders

Lebanon contains all types of beautiful wonders, from beautiful mountains, to unreal grottos, and magical valleys, to white sandy beaches, to vast waterfalls and paths, to historical remains and architecure.

If you are planning to come to Lebanon for a trip, these aare the places i'd look first, neatly wrapped and stored in organized boxes to help you plan your trip efficiently!

HISTORY

  • Baalbek

    Located in North-East Lebanon, Baalbek is filled with Roman history that's more beautiful than the rest and has thought-proking stories and tales. If you plan on seeing historical sights, my first recommendation is Baalbek. From the Temple of Jupiter, to the Temple of Bacchus, and other beautiful Roman architecture.

  • Deir el-Qamar

    Located in the heart of Mount Lebanon, Deir el Qamar feels less like a typical tourist stop and more like an open-air time capsule. It is a historic, stone-paved town that once served as the capital of the Mount Lebanon Emirate. From Al Midan Square, to Palace of Emir Fakhreddine II, or Marie Baz Museum, etc. I highly recommend visiting Deir El-Qamar if you are interested in observing centuries of Lebanese political and cultural History.

  • Beiteddine

    Located in the heart of Mount Lebanon as well, Beiteddine is a masterful display of 19th-century Lebanese architecture, luxury, and intricate craftsmanship. It is a breathtaking palace complex that served as a headquarters for a dynasty. From the grand outer courtyards, to the mosaic museum, etc. I highly recommend going to Beiteddine to visit if you want to experience the height of royal historic living.

CITIES

  • Tyre

    Located on the southern coast of Lebanon, Tyre is a legendary Phoenician maritime city filled with ancient seafaring lore and breathtaking archaeological ruins. It once ruled the Mediterranean trade networks and famously resisted Alexander the Great's seige. From the Roman Hippodrome, to the bustling old fishermen's port, or Al Mina archaeological site, etc. If you are interested in Roman history and love wide beaches, Tyre is a perfect city for you to visit!

  • Sidon

    Similarly to Tyre, Sidon is located in southern Lebanon and is a Phoenician port city with maritime trade legacy and is a part of the Crusader-era. It is a vibrant coastal hub that served as a gateway of commerce for millenia. From the iconic Sea Castle rising out of the sea, to the labyrinthe-esque souqs, or the historic Soap Museum, it a noteworthy location to visit for you to explore history and the modern-age!

  • Byblos

    Located in the northern coast of Lebanon, Byblos is considered the first ever port in the world, hence it's considered one of the oldest inhabited cities in the world, layered wiht thousands of years of civilizations. It is a charming coastal city where Phoenician, Roman, and Crusader ruins sit right alongside a lively modern nightlife. From the ancient Crusader castle, to the old harbor, or the atmospheric stone souqs, etc. Byblos is a landmark for any history enthusiast or modern nightlife enjoyer, blending deep ancient history and a vibrant seaside culture.

NATURE

  • Jeita Grotto

    Located in the heart of Mount Lebanon, Jeita Grotto is a breathtaking natural wonder of spectacular limestone formations. It is a famous, two-tiered cave system sculpted by water over millions over years. From the colossal stalactites of the upper cave, to the surreal boat ride across the lower river, etc. Jeita Grotto is a must-visit to witness nature's ultimate artistry.

  • Qadisha Valley

    Located in northern Lebanon, the Qadisha Valley is a mythical, deep-cut gorge filled with profound spiritual heritage and dramatic natural beauty. It is a sanctuary that served as a refuge for Christian communities for centuries. From the ancient monasteries carved into sheer cliff faces, to the quiet hiking trails, etc. It perfectly blends deep serenity with raw mountain scenery.

  • el-Chouf Cedar Nature Reserve

    Located in the rugged heights of Mountain Lebanon, the Chouf Cedar Nature Reserve is a majestic ecological sanctuary brimming with pristine wilderness. It is the country's largest reserve, protecting iconic, centuries-old, Lebanese Cedars that once supplied ancient empires with timber. From the panoramic mountain viewpoints, to the extensive network of hiking trails, etc. It is the perfect destination for any hiking lover.
